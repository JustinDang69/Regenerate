Microneedling

The Collagen Booster

A minimally invasive skin-needling treatment designed to support skin renewal, texture and collagen-related remodelling through controlled micro-channels.

Book Now →
Treatment pathway

What the treatment is designed to support

Concern

Texture, the appearance of certain scars, and skin rejuvenation goals.

Technology

Sterile fine needles create controlled microscopic channels in the skin as part of a practitioner-led treatment.

Benefit

Designed to support skin renewal and a smoother, revitalised-looking appearance. Individual response and suitability vary.

Held back from public copy — evidence + legal review The source document states micro-channels improve product absorption “by up to 300% to 500%”. That figure is not shown above. Ahpra advertising guidance prohibits claims that create unreasonable expectations of benefit, so this needs supporting evidence and clinical/legal sign-off before it could appear publicly.
Your appointment

What to expect

Before

  • A consultation with your practitioner, including a health-history review.
  • Skin assessment and a discussion of your goals.
  • Before-and-after photography, only with your consent.
  • Questions are welcome at any point.

During

  • Numbing cream is applied around 30 minutes beforehand.
  • Controlled skin needling is performed by your practitioner.
  • Comfort levels vary between individuals and are discussed beforehand.

After

  • Mild to moderate redness is expected and typically settles within about two days.
  • Dryness or a feeling of tightness may follow, often easing around days 4–5.
  • Many people notice a change once flaking has settled.
Aftercare

Looking after your skin

Days 0–2
  • Avoid touching the face, direct sun, strenuous exercise, swimming, sauna and steam.
  • Avoid make-up.
Days 0–4
  • Use a gentle cleanser, a hyaluronic-acid serum and moisturiser.
  • Apply mineral SPF and reapply regularly.
Days 5–7
  • Active serums such as vitamin C can be reintroduced gradually.
Recommended schedule

A course of at least 3–6 sessions, spaced 4–6 weeks apart, with maintenance around every 3–4 months. Your plan is confirmed in consultation.

Suitability

Suitability, expected experience and an individual plan are confirmed during consultation. Results vary from person to person.

Mesotherapy — one page, two sections

Face Mesotherapy

Micro-injections of skin boosters, serums and nourishing components into the middle layer of the skin — designed to support glow, skin laxity, the appearance of fine lines and evenness of tone, depending on the products selected.

  • Numbing cream applied around 30 minutes beforehand.
  • Mild pinching or pricking is expected, particularly in thinner-skinned areas.
  • Redness, swelling and small bumps usually settle within a day; tiny needle marks may show for 24–48 hours.

Scalp Mesotherapy

The same micro-injection approach applied to the scalp, delivering selected minerals and amino acids close to the hair follicle as part of a consultation-led hair-support plan.

  • Do not wash the hair for 24 hours.
  • Avoid scratching or putting pressure on the scalp.
  • Avoid hair dye and chemical treatments for one week.
  • Sleep on a clean pillowcase.
Removed from public copy — TGA The source document names two prescription medicines in connection with scalp mesotherapy. Those names are deliberately absent above. TGA guidance is that prescription medicines generally cannot be advertised to the public, and promoting a health service by referring to them — directly or indirectly — can itself become prohibited advertising. Kept as an internal clinical note only.

HydraFacial — five-step process

CleanseMakeup, oil and surface impurities are removed.
ExfoliateGentle exfoliation lifts dead skin cells.
ExtractVacuum-assisted extraction loosens impurities from pores.
Hydrate & InfuseHydrating ingredients selected for your skin.
ProtectMoisturiser and broad-spectrum sunscreen to finish.
First 24–48 hours
  • Avoid excessive heat, saunas and steam.
  • Avoid intense exercise if the skin feels sensitive.
  • Avoid harsh exfoliants unless advised otherwise.
  • Use gentle products and daily SPF 30+.
Typical frequency

Commonly around every 4–6 weeks, personalised to your skin. More intensive goals may be spaced every 2–4 weeks; longer-term maintenance every 1–3 months.
